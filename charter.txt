Project Justification  

A brief summary of the project that includes reference to the commissioning organisation the target end users and providing a justification for initiating the project and the perceived key benefits. Think of the project from the perspective of your customer, this is not about what you want to achieve on the project, but what your customer wants the project to deliver and why. 

 

A customer making air purifiers and wanting to show proof that they work effectively in making the air quality better in a closed room. We will have 3 different sensors that monitor the air flow, temperature, and humidity and quality. We will have data to prove that the air purifier works effectively by collecting data before the purifier is on and after it is on. This will prove that the purifier is effective or not.

Help customer see how well their product, the air purifier, works. From the data we provide, they can use it to develop the product further and make changes as they see fit.  

 

Help customer measure the air flow, temperature, and quality through the data we provide them. To measure this, we will have a database to pull from and display data on a website. 

 

Help customer gain data in different closed environments which they can then use to develop their product, the air purifier. 

 

It is realistic as every member of the team has assigned tasks in their area and we have a project manager to help in any area if anyone needs the support as well as means of communication to ask each other on discord for assistance or in the weekly meetings. 

 

The project deadline is on the 5th of May 2025. We have about 11 weeks to complete the project. We have a goal of about 5 hours a week each to work on the project. 25 hours a week. 275 hours on the project total.
